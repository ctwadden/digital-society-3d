## Textbook statements to check before teaching

These are flagged from reading the chapter. None has been verified against an external source in
this session. Each needs a dated authoritative source in the update log before a slide states a
correction.

| Page | Textbook statement | Concern | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 93, 101 | Moore's Law given as doubling every two years, dated to 1965 | Moore's 1965 paper is usually reported as predicting yearly doubling, revised to about two years in 1975 | Verify, then teach the two-stage history |
| 101 | Photo captioned as a UNIVAC computer "from the 1940s"; first generation dated 1940–46 | UNIVAC I is usually dated to 1951; generation date ranges vary by source | Verify; teach the dates as approximate |
| 102 | Fifth generation linked to "superconductors (with millions of transistors on a microchip)" | Wording mixes separate ideas; modern chips hold billions of transistors | Verify and restate plainly |
| 93 | Laptop sales passed desktops by July 2005 | Scope (which country, retail or all sales) is not stated | Verify scope or present as a textbook claim |
| 94 | Device-use table for 2019 and 2020 | Old data; percentages for 2020 do not obviously sum to 100 | Do not reuse. Build practice from current, sourced figures or clearly labelled classroom data |
| 95 | Underwater data centre launched 2018, "eight times more reliable" | Project has since ended; current status matters to the judgement | Verify outcome and date |
| 95 | Data centres use about 200 TWh a year, 0.3 % of emissions, ICT over 2 % | Figures are dated and estimates vary widely, especially with AI demand | Replace with a current sourced range and state uncertainty |
| 98 | 5.6 billion malware attacks in 2020; 200 malicious apps removed in September 2021 | Dated statistics and case | Keep as a dated historical case or replace with a current one |
| 103 | Quantum computing uses listed as being researched | Students may read these as achieved | Teach as proposed uses; verify any named company case |
